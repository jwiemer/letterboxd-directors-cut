Director’s Cut

A little more behind every film.

Trailers, behind the scenes, photo galleries, and more ratings, right on Letterboxd in Safari.

Set up in Safari

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions. On iOS 17, start at Settings → Safari.
  2. Turn on Director’s Cut.
  3. Open Letterboxd in Safari. In the page menu, choose Manage Extensions and allow Director’s Cut on Letterboxd.

If ratings are missing, open Director’s Cut from Safari’s page menu and allow the rating sites you want to use.

Open Letterboxd

Use the website in Safari. This extension doesn’t change the Letterboxd app. Your settings are saved on this device.
